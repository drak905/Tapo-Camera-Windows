Using the app

A short tour of the main window. Sign in first: see the guides for Tapo, Kami and iMou.

The grid

The buttons at the top (1×1 to 5×5) set how many tiles the grid shows: 1, 4, 9, 16 or 25. The app remembers your choice. The first time it uses 2×2.

Every camera that is visible in the grid plays at the same time. A camera that is not visible does not play. Tiles show these texts while there is no picture: Connecting…, Reconnecting… or Offline. The grid uses a smaller stream so many tiles can run at once.

Click a tile to select it. The side panels then show that camera.

Choosing which camera is where

The grid has 25 places (slots). A 3×3 grid shows the first 9 of them. An empty slot shows Empty and the hint “Right-click or drag a camera here”. An empty slot never plays video.

  • Drag and drop: drag a camera from the camera list onto a tile. You can also drag one tile onto another. If the camera already has a slot, the two slots swap.
  • Tile menu (right-click a tile): Remove from view, Show camera (a list of all cameras; the one you pick goes into this slot), Full screen and Rename….
  • List menu (right-click a camera in the list): Add to view, Remove from view, Rename… and Reset to camera name.

Cameras that are not in the current view are shown in grey italics in the list. A new camera takes the first empty slot. A camera you removed from the view stays out until you add it again. The slots are remembered between starts.

Full screen

Double-click a tile, or choose Full screen in its menu. Only that camera plays, in a larger, sharper stream. Double-click again to go back to the grid.

Side panels and shortcuts

The Cameras panel on the left lists your cameras. The Camera panel on the right has two tabs: Controls and Playback.

KeysWhat it does
Ctrl+BShow or hide the camera list (also in the View menu).
Ctrl+JShow or hide the Controls / Playback panel (also in the View menu).
W A S D or the arrow keysMove the selected camera up, left, down or right while you hold the key. Only for Tapo cameras that can pan and tilt.
F2Rename the selected camera.

Hiding a panel gives its space to the grid. It does not start or stop any video. The app remembers which panels are hidden.

The Controls tab is for Tapo cameras: privacy mode, status LED, siren, motion detection, night vision (auto, IR on, IR off), the pan/tilt pad, saved positions (presets) and a Snapshot button. A snapshot is saved as a PNG file in a folder under your Pictures folder, and the status bar shows the exact path. Kami and iMou cameras have no controls.

Recordings: the player and the timeline

Open the Playback tab. Pick a day. For a Tapo camera the app reads the list of clips from the memory card; each row shows the start and end time in your local time. Press Play (or double-click a clip) to open the player window. Press Download clip… to save the selected clip as an MP4 file.

The player window opens one per camera. Closing it stops the playback. It has:

  • A 24-hour timeline for the day, with the recorded parts marked and a moving cursor. Click or drag on it to jump to a time.
  • Play / pause, Previous clip and Next clip.
  • A speed box: 1x, 2x, 4x and 8x for Tapo cameras. The faster speeds have been tested less than normal speed. iMou recordings play at 1x only.
  • A date box. Changing the day stops playback and loads that day.
  • Snapshot and Download clip….

Recordings work for Tapo cameras (at home and away) and, watch only, for iMou cameras (see the iMou guide). Kami cameras show “Recordings are not supported for this Kami camera”.

Sorting and renaming

Sort. The Sort button above the camera list has three choices: Name A–Z (the default), Name Z–A and Account order. Numbers sort naturally, so “Cam 2” comes before “Cam 10”. Sorting changes only the list. The grid keeps its own layout.

Rename. Select a camera and press F2, or use Rename… in a menu. A name must have 1 to 32 bytes, so letters with accents count as 2 or 3 bytes. The dialog tells you where the new name goes:

  • Your own Tapo camera: the name is saved on the camera. It also shows in the Tapo app and for everyone the camera is shared with. The cloud can take a while to show it.
  • A shared Tapo camera, a camera added by IP address, a Kami camera or an iMou camera: the name is used only in this app. For a shared Tapo camera, Reset to camera name brings back the name from the cloud.

Tray icon and notifications

  • Closing the window hides it to the tray by default, and video keeps running for Tapo cameras. iMou tiles stop while the window is hidden or minimized, to save quota. Double-click or click the tray icon, or choose Show in its menu, to bring the window back.
  • To quit, right-click the tray icon and choose Quit. This stops all streams. If your system has no tray, closing the window quits the app.
  • Starting the app a second time does not open a second copy. It brings the first window forward.
  • Motion notifications: when a Tapo camera reports motion, Windows shows a notification with the camera name and the time. There is at most one per camera every 10 seconds. The camera’s Motion detection switch must be on. Kami and iMou cameras send no motion notifications.

Hiding to the tray and notifications are on by default. The app has no switch for them. They are the settings minimize_to_tray and notifications in the settings block of config.json in the data folder. Close the app before you edit that file.

The data folder

The app keeps its files in %APPDATA%\TapoDesktop. The name is unchanged from earlier versions so that your settings and sign-ins carry over. To open it, paste the path into the Windows Explorer address bar.

FileWhat is in it
config.jsonSettings, the list of cameras, the grid slots. No passwords.
vault.jsonSaved passwords, sessions and keys, protected by Windows so that only your Windows user can read them.
pins.jsonRemembered security certificates of your cameras.
tapo-desktop.logThe log. It does not contain passwords or tokens.

Do not copy vault.json to another PC or another Windows user. It cannot be read there.

Signing out

Open the account button in the top bar. Each account type has its own entries and signing out of one does not touch the others:

  • Tapo: Sign out. Removes your cloud cameras and forgets your TP-Link ID password. Cameras added by IP address stay.
  • Kami: Sign out of Kami. Removes the Kami cameras and forgets the session.
  • iMou (Open Platform): Disconnect iMou. Removes the iMou cameras and the saved App ID and secret.

To forget everything, quit the app from the tray and delete the data folder. This cannot be undone: you will have to sign in again and add manual cameras again.

If video or downloads fail

  • Web filters such as AdGuard can intercept plain HTTP traffic from programs on your PC, including traffic to your cameras. Exclude TepoDesktop.exe and ffmpeg.exe in the filter’s settings.
  • Tapo cameras use a media port that speaks plain HTTP. A filter that blocks or rewrites it breaks live view and downloads.
  • A tile that says Offline means the app could not get video. Use Refresh cameras in the account menu and check the camera in its own app.
